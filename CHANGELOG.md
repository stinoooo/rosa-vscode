# Changelog

## 1.3.0

- Your sprout has a love bar. Click it to give it a pet, and a little heart
  floats up.
- Love empties over a day. A well-loved sprout sends hearts on its own, and a
  lonely one asks for a pet.

## 1.2.0

- Your sprout grows from real work: a save counts when it changed the file,
  and one file counts at most once a minute.
- Typing keeps your sprout awake.
- Every stage has its own drawing, from a big-headed hatchling to an adult
  with back plates.
- The egg rocks from the start, cracks as it gets close, and breaks open when
  it hatches.
- Once hatched, your sprout gets hungry and thirsty. Saves feed it, and
  clearing your errors gives it water. It never dies or loses growth.
- The status bar shows an egg until it hatches.

## 1.1.0

- Rosa Product Icons and Rosa File Icons.
- Your sprout, a pixel sauropod in the Explorer that grows as you save.

## 1.0.0
